iBO QuestionnaireToolkit add-on - Third-Party Notices
=====================================================

This add-on contains QuestionnaireToolkit ((c) Pascal Jansen), adapted for Interactive Bayesian
Optimization (iBO), and the questionnaire example scenes. The third-party components below keep their own
terms. The iBO core package lists its own components in Assets/iBO/Documentation/Third-Party Notices.txt.
